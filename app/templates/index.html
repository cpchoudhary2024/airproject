PM2.5 Correction Formula Comparison

Comparison of correction equations for raw sensor readings. Primary analysis uses EPA Barkjohn (2021).

EPA Barkjohn (2021) PM2.5 = 0.524 * raw - 0.0862 * RH + 5.75 US EPA nationwide correction for ambient PurpleAir data; relative humidity corrected.
LRAPA PM2.5 = 0.5 * raw - 0.66 Lane Regional Air Protection Agency. Linear adjustment without RH term.
AQ&U PM2.5 = 0.778 * raw + 2.65 University of Utah AQ&U network correction.

    Methods & Standards

    Analytical metrics are derived from peer-reviewed literature and environmental health standards.

    WHO 2021: 24-hour PM2.5 15 µg/m³ Air Quality Guideline (health-based)
    WHO 2021: annual PM2.5 5 µg/m³ Long-term exposure guideline
    US EPA: 24-hour NAAQS 35 µg/m³ Regulatory standard
    US EPA: annual NAAQS (2024) 9.0 µg/m³ Revised from 12.0 in 2024
    1. Barkjohn, K.K., Gantt, B., & Clements, A.L. (2021). Development and application of a United States-wide correction for PM2.5 data collected with the PurpleAir sensor. Atmospheric Measurement Techniques, 14(6), 4617-4637. doi:10.5194/amt-14-4617-2021 Default correction equation, developed from US ambient outdoor data.
    2. World Health Organization (2021). WHO global air quality guidelines: particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide and carbon monoxide. Geneva: WHO. who.int Source of the WHO 15 µg/m³ health guideline.
    3. U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (2024). National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) for Particulate Matter: Final Reconsideration. epa.gov Source of the EPA 35 µg/m³ (24-h) and 9.0 µg/m³ (annual) standards.
    4. Cleveland, R.B., Cleveland, W.S., McRae, J.E., & Terpenning, I. (1990). STL: A Seasonal-Trend Decomposition Procedure Based on Loess. Journal of Official Statistics, 6(1), 3-73. Basis of the trend, seasonality, and residual decomposition.
    5. Lane Regional Air Protection Agency (LRAPA). PurpleAir correction factor.  ·  Sayahi, T., Butterfield, A., & Kelly, K.E. (AQ&U, Univ. of Utah). Low-cost sensor calibration. Alternative correction formulas in comparison panel.

    Where an "AQI" value is shown it is an illustrative PM2.5 sub-index only. The official U.S. AQI considers other pollutants, and real-time AQI uses EPA NowCast rather than a single sensor reading. WHO and EPA 24-hour values apply to daily PM2.5 concentrations; crossings by individual readings are reference comparisons, not formal exceedances.
