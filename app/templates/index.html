Quality Framework & Methodology

1. Quality Score (JHU/MIT Standard)

Formula: QS = 0.4×Validity + 0.6×Coverage

Weights temporal completeness (60%) and reading validity (40%) so that both silent gaps and bad readings reduce the score.

2. Sensor Health Index (CV)

Formula: CV = (MAD / Mean PM2.5) × 100

EXCELLENT (CV < 10%) | ACCEPTED (10-15%) | INVALID (≥15%)

3. STL Decomposition

Period = 720 observations (2-min intervals = 24h cycle)

Separates trend, seasonality, and residuals to identify pollution events independent of diurnal patterns.

4. Gap Analysis

Distinguishes contiguous vs stochastic gaps

Contiguous gaps (e.g., 8+ hours) are more damaging to trend analysis than random blips.

PM2.5 Correction Formula Comparison

Compare how three correction equations translate raw sensor readings. The main analysis uses EPA Barkjohn.

EPA Barkjohn (2021) PM2.5 = 0.534 × raw − 0.0844 × RH + 5.604 Standard for PurpleAir sensors; humidity-corrected. Used for all main analysis.
LRAPA PM2.5 = 0.5 × raw − 0.66 Lane Regional Air Protection Agency. Simple linear, no RH correction.
AQ&U PM2.5 = 0.778 × raw + 2.65 University of Utah / AQ&U network correction.
